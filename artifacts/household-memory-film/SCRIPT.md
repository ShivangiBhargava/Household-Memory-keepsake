# Household Memory / Keepsake — Film Script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A home runs on small details; Keepsake gives everyone one shared, attributable place to put them.
- **Identity:** Keepsake's existing warm archival palette, extended into deep pine, paper-cream, muted copper, and soft sage. Instrument Serif for intimate editorial headlines; DM Sans for interface copy; IBM Plex Mono for protocol details.
- **Hero:** The saved memory itself, transformed from loose household scraps into a shared, searchable record.
- **Motion:** Paper-to-interface match cuts, a virtual camera that dives through memory cards, and a traced MCP request path.
- **Arc:** Domestic friction snaps into focus; the pace becomes orderly as details acquire context; the finale pulls back to reveal a self-hosted system rather than a magical assistant.
- **Payoff:** The scattered details from the opener resolve into a single connected household record, then the film loops by matching its final memory tile back to the opening paper fragment.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9, 30 seconds, five scenes of 6 seconds each.
- **Claims discipline:** Demo names and sample records are explicitly fictional and not authenticated. The Alexa+ client is a simulation, not an Alexa+ Preview connection. No external account, endorsement, or product-performance claim is implied.

## Fully scripted shots

### Shot 01 / 00.00–06.00 / 6.00s — “THE LITTLE THINGS”

**Purpose:** Make the familiar low-grade stress of household details instantly recognizable, then establish a place to hold them.

**Exact content and holds:** 00.00: small mono label `HOUSEHOLD MEMORY`; 00.35: `WHERE DID WE PUT…` appears in large editorial type; 00.65–1.10: three scraps successively read `PASSPORT?`, `WARRANTY?`, `WHO'S ON CHORES?`; 1.35–3.20: headline resolves to `The little things.`; 3.20–4.75: supporting copy `Easy to lose. Useful to remember.`; 4.80–6.00: paper card carries `ONE SHARED MEMORY` and tiny `FICTIONAL DEMO`.

**Composition:** Start on textured cream paper filling the 16:9 frame, macro crop. Copper scribble left, torn sage paper upper right, pale blue-grey paper lower center. Headline owns the central-left, scraps orbit its perimeter; 5% safe inset. Finish with one centered, deep-pine memory tile occupying roughly half the short edge.

**Artistic idea:** Everyday paper ephemera physically folds into the digital card—one continuous material transformation, not a text card replacing another.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 a thin copper pencil line draws left-to-right; 0.18 scraps snap in with stiff springs, each 0.44s apart; 0.55–1.35 type punches on, with the three paper prompts hitting at 0.65 / 0.87 / 1.09; 1.45 camera pulls out to reveal the fragments orbiting the question; 2.25 a paper corner curls, 2.60 scraps rotate into alignment; 3.15 the phrase `The little things.` settles into a calm central-left block; 4.20 the scraps fold inward and cast one rectangular shadow; 5.20 that fold resolves as a memory tile, beginning the push-through to scene 2. At least one visible change every 0.5–0.8 seconds; no empty tail.

**Transition contract:** The rectangular tile finishes the fold at frame center-right. Scene 2 opens on that exact tile at 1.45x scale and 10px blur, with an outlined second tile already visible; camera settles while the textured field divides into a clean paper-colored grid.

**Assets/technique:** No external asset. CSS/SVG paper grains, ruled lines, card edges, and a clipped paper fold; Instrument Serif / DM Sans / IBM Plex Mono.

**Sound intent:** Lo-fi instrumental; first sharp accent at 0.00, paper-snap accents across the 0.4-second opening rhythm. Beat alignment to be measured after audio generation.

**Artistic idea:** The categories are not icons on a list: their tabs slide out of a single card's folded edge, then the card multiplies into a spatial index.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.65 camera settles from the previous push-through; 0.35–1.15 tabs punch out one-by-one (location / warranty / chore); 1.10–2.15 cards spawn from depth at 0.28s intervals and rotate to a shallow 3D angle; 2.20 the camera pulls back to reveal three categories within one shelf; 3.10 a small copper date stamp flips on the washer card; 4.05 the chore recurrence traces itself in a loop; 4.85 camera dives toward the Maya attribution line, the line expanding to full frame for scene 3.

**Assets/technique:** Built interface cards and SVG shelf/timeline geometry only. Fictional examples explicitly marked; represent categories and fields available in the app.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues. Three quick percussive accents on the category tabs; cut to shot 3 on the nearest measured onset.

### Shot 03 / 12.00–18.00 / 6.00s — “WHO CHANGED WHAT”

**Purpose:** Make the provenance feature emotionally legible: shared details include who added or changed them, and when.

**Exact content and holds:** 12.00–12.80: `MAYA · ADDED` and `THEO · UPDATED` appear on separate timeline nodes; 13.25: `Every detail has a history.`; 14.20–15.65 close-up event readout `Maya added “Passport”` / `Today · 9:14 AM`; 15.85–16.95 `Theo updated “Washer warranty”` / `Yesterday · 6:42 PM`; 17.15–17.80 `Demo names · not verified accounts`.

**Composition:** Begin wide and centered on a horizontal copper timeline, with two large name nodes at far left and right. Pull into the first node so its event receipt fills the left two-thirds, then a brisk pivot to the second node. Background switches to an untextured muted sage plane, unlike the paper textures of shots 1–2. Names are demo labels, not account identities.

**Artistic idea:** The timeline is a single continuous drawn thread; on each event it briefly tightens into an underline beneath the exact changed record, proving attribution is attached to the memory.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 axis wipes open from the previous copper line; 0.25–0.90 two nodes bloom on distinct beats; 1.10 a tiny timestamp counter ticks into `9:14 AM`; 1.35–2.05 headline reveals through a horizontal mask; 2.20 camera pushes 1.25x into Maya's record as its underline draws; 3.05 line zips to the Theo node, camera follows in a 0.45s whip; 3.65 the second timestamp flips from `—` to `Yesterday · 6:42 PM`; 4.55 the attribution disclaimer appears beneath both events; 5.20 line exits frame right, carrying into the query underline. Events remain legible through the exit.

**Transition contract:** Copper line exits to the right and reappears under the incoming question. Scene 4 opens on a split screen, left chat panel with the same baseline underline; the perspective panel unfold and blurred incoming query resolve together.

**Assets/technique:** Drawn timeline, timestamp counter values scheduled through `useSceneTimer`, geometric panels, no photography. All sample names/timestamps fictional.

**Sound intent:** Lo-fi bed adds a light tick at each recorded action; 3.05s pivot hits a measured onset if available.

### Shot 04 / 18.00–24.00 / 6.00s — “ASK. FIND. CITE.”

**Purpose:** Demonstrate the retrieval flow without implying a real Alexa+ Preview connection.

**Exact content and holds:** 18.00 permanent label `SIMULATED ALEXA+ WEB CLIENT`; 18.25 query types `Where did we put the passport?`; 19.20 `SEARCHING SAVED MEMORIES`; 19.80 result resolves `Passport` / `Hall closet`; 20.35 source line `Added by Maya · Today, 9:14 AM`; 21.45–23.20 headline `An answer with its source.`; 23.20–24.00 bottom label `SIMULATION · NOT AN ALEXA+ PREVIEW CONNECTION`.

**Composition:** Two pane composition: left quarter contains a restrained speech waveform and the query; right three-quarters contains an initially closed, concentric memory aperture around a large fictional result record. The category label stays above the copy; source line stays below the answer. Pine field, paper result, thin sage border. Preserve 5% margins.

**Artistic idea:** The query's underline becomes a search path that enters a radial aperture and physically reveals the one saved card, then remains attached to its provenance.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 split panels unfold on the copper line while the result aperture opens from 4% to 100%; 0.30 query types as a two-beat glyph reveal; 1.15 path sweeps from left panel through aperture; 1.75 result card scales 0.68→1.04→1.00 in 0.8s; 2.25 location line illuminates, then source line traces beneath it; 3.35 the headline replaces the query in the left panel; 4.30 disclaimer grows crisp at the bottom; 5.05 camera pushes through the card's thin green border while an MCP route label begins resolving, handing off in motion.

**Transition contract:** The thick result border expands past frame as a push-through, with the result's shadow fading separately. Scene 5 starts from a matching ring at 1.5x/10px blur; a route label is already visible at 0.15s as the plane resolves to a dark pine network field.

**Assets/technique:** CSS chat/result artwork, SVG signal trace and radial aperture. No actual Alexa integration shown; exact disclaimer retained.

**Sound intent:** Smooth percussive pulse carries the search path; one gentle low hit at result resolution, no generated SFX.
